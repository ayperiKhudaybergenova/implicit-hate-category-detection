# implicit-hate-category-detection

Grievance — Complaining or blaming a group for a problem.
Incitement — Encouraging people to act against a group.
Inferiority — Saying or implying that a group is less capable or less worthy.
Irony — Using irony, sarcasm, humor, or satire to demean a group.
Stereotypes — Linking a group to a negative general belief or characteristic.
Threats — Suggesting or expressing harm toward a group.
Other — Implicit hate that does not clearly fit into the other categories.
